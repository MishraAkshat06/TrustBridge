# TrustBridge — Research Notes

1. Crowdfunding success prediction using ML

3. Numerical + textual campaign prediction

5. Investor dynamics
6. Blockchain crowdfunding
7. Smart-contract escrow and automated fund management

## Initial literature findings discussed

### Elitzur et al. (2024)
Research on machine-learning methods for crowdfunding success prediction. It establishes that ML success prediction is already an active research direction.

**TrustBridge use:** methodology and baseline design; do not claim success prediction itself is novel.

### Ardakani et al. (2024/2025)
Research on crowdfunding storytellers/project success using numerical and textual features and multiple ML/NLP approaches.

**TrustBridge use:** supports campaign-description/text features and future NLP experiments.

### Feng et al. (2024)
Feature-selection-based crowdfunding prediction research.

**TrustBridge use:** useful for feature selection and compact predictive models.

### Perez et al. (2020)
“I call BS: Fraud Detection in Crowdfunding Campaigns.”

**TrustBridge use:** directly relevant to campaign-risk/fraud assessment before contributors fund a campaign.

### Porro et al. (2024/2025)
Research on investor dynamics in equity/lending crowdfunding using ML and temporal approaches.

**TrustBridge use:** possible future direction for dynamic campaign-risk updates.

### Gafrej et al. (2026)
Research on explainable ML for technology reward crowdfunding using SHAP.

**TrustBridge use:** supports explainability and motivates an Agentic AI explanation layer.

### Blockchain crowdfunding research
Recent work studies blockchain-based crowdfunding, smart contracts, traceability, automation and decentralized fund management.

**TrustBridge use:** supports the smart-contract escrow and programmable-fund-control layer.

## Research conclusion
Individual components are already researched. Do not claim that ML crowdfunding prediction or blockchain crowdfunding alone is novel.

A stronger candidate gap is:

> Integration of quantitative campaign prediction, anomaly/risk analysis, Agentic AI explanation/evidence review, creator verification and programmable milestone-based blockchain escrow.

This must be validated with a systematic literature review before being stated as a definitive novelty claim.

## Research questions
- Can ML predict crowdfunding success from launch-time information?
- Can Agentic AI improve interpretability and usefulness?
- Can anomaly detection identify risky campaigns without excessive false positives?
- Can smart contracts reliably enforce hard caps and milestone releases?
- Does the integrated architecture improve transparency and programmable fund control?
