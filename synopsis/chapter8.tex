\chapter{Conclusion}

TrustBridge proposes and will evaluate an integrated crowdfunding architecture that addresses two fundamental limitations of existing platforms: the inability of contributors to objectively assess campaign quality before funding, and the lack of programmable accountability mechanisms after funding.

By combining ML-based success prediction, anomaly-driven risk detection, Agentic AI explanation and evidence review, off-chain creator verification, and Solidity smart-contract escrow with milestone-based staged fund release, TrustBridge creates a system in which transparency is data-driven, accountability is code-enforced, and trust is built into the protocol rather than assumed from the platform.

The project is grounded in a substantive body of related work across ML crowdfunding prediction, blockchain escrow, and explainable AI. Its research contribution lies in the integration and empirical evaluation of all these components in a single, end-to-end system — a gap confirmed by the literature review in Chapter 5.

The 12-week implementation plan, beginning with a 4-week POC phase on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet, is designed to progressively validate each system component before full integration. The POC phase alone will produce on-chain evidence of correct hard-cap enforcement, partial contribution refunds, milestone-gated withdrawal, and automatic refund execution — demonstrating the core TrustBridge value proposition with verifiable data.

Upon completion, TrustBridge will be a research-ready prototype suitable for academic evaluation, supervisor demonstration, and as the basis of a conference or journal paper contribution. It is expected to demonstrate that an integrated AI-plus-blockchain architecture can meaningfully improve both the pre-funding decision quality and the post-funding accountability of crowdfunding campaigns.
